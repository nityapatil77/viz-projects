# touch designer

a collection of generative visual experiments built in TouchDesigner — exploring particle systems, analog signal manipulation, and machine learning–driven interaction.

---

## [rainbow flow](https://youtu.be/9PBZAfLtAvs)

Using math mix, attributes, and RGB colorways, particles are constrained to move based on randomness. Layering blur and chromatic aberration over the output produces an effect that sits somewhere between fluid simulation and light diffraction — chaotic but controlled.

**concepts:** particle systems · RGB color math · chromatic aberration · procedural motion

---

## [video mix](https://youtu.be/kLi9G4XV2SE)

A real-time video manipulation experiment rooted in circuit bending logic. By manipulating voltage levels and visual signal data, brightness and color shift live — treating video as a malleable, physical material rather than a fixed output.

**concepts:** circuit bending · real-time signal processing · voltage-driven color manipulation

---

## [hand tracking](https://youtu.be/wX5gsCTIRfc)

A machine learning–powered interaction where finger position drives wave and radial text animations. The scale of amplification is visualized directly — making the hand not just a controller, but part of the composition itself.

**concepts:** ML hand tracking · visual overlay · gesture-to-parameter mapping · generative typography
